# Experiments

## synthetic_d2

Measures how closely generated samples match an exact posterior marginal in the smallest synthetic setting. This is the minimum core-claim experiment.

## synthetic_d5

Measures whether posterior recovery remains effective as nuisance dimensions are added while scoring the same two-dimensional marginal.

## synthetic_d10

Measures the harder synthetic setting from the paper's main table, where posterior sampling must remain accurate with more latent coordinates.
